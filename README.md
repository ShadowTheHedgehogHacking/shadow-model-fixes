## About

This repo contains model fixes for the original Shadow the Hedgehog game.


Feel free to distribute or use these in your own mods as you wish.


They are already included by default in Shadow the Hedgehog: Reloaded v1.2.

## What issues are fixed? - GameCube
* The Yellow Android's left hand material stops rendering when a weapon is held
* The Pink Android's weapon extension material is not rendering
* Artificial Chaos eye glow is incorrectly rotated 90 degrees
* BigFoot Type B unit cockpit glare attaches to Shadow's relative camera instead of the unit [GameCube exclusive bug]
* HPBars have a stray pixel below every health bar [GameCube exclusive bug]

## What issues are fixed? - Xbox
* Artificial Chaos eye glow is incorrectly rotated 90 degrees
* BigFoot cockpit transparency is broken [Xbox exclusive bug]
